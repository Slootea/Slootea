We get it — finding the right time to talk to a team can be a hassle. Between busy schedules, different time zones, and endless email threads, it sometimes feels like booking a simple meeting shouldn't be this hard.

Well, good news: **it doesn't have to be.**

## We're Here When You Need Us

Whether you have a question about Slootea, want a quick walkthrough of the platform, or just want to say hi, our team is ready to chat. And the best part? You don't need to wait for business hours or send an email and hope for a reply.

Just pick a time that works for you, and we'll be there.

## How It Works

Booking an appointment with us is as easy as it gets:

1. Click the **Meet the Team** button below
2. Pick a date and time that works for you
3. Show up — we'll handle the rest

No forms to fill out, no waiting in a queue. Just a straightforward way to connect with real people who care about helping you get the most out of Slootea.

## What Can We Help With?

Here are a few things people usually book a call for:

- **Getting started** — We'll walk you through everything so you feel right at home
- **Questions about features** — Curious about something? Let's talk it through
- **Custom setup help** — Need things configured a certain way? We've got you
- **Feedback and ideas** — We genuinely love hearing what you think

## No Pressure, Just a Conversation

This isn't a sales call. It's a friendly chat with the people building Slootea. We want to understand what you need and see if we can help. That's it.

So go ahead — grab a slot and let's talk. We're looking forward to meeting you.
